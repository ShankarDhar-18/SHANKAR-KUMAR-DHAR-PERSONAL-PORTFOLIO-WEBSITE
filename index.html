Projects

A STUDY ON THE BEHAVIOUR OF ASPIRANTS FOR CHOOSING ACADEMIC STREAM USING POWER BI

This project explores the key factors influencing students when selecting their academic streams such as Science, Commerce or Arts. Using survey data, I analyzed patterns based on interests, parental influence, career goals and academic performance. With the help of Power BI I created interactive dashboards to visualize trends and uncover insights into decision-making behavior. The analysis highlights how different variables impact student choices and helps identify common trends among aspirants. This project provides valuable insights for educators, counselors and policymakers to better understand student preferences and guide them in making informed decisions about their academic paths.

DOCTOR APPOINTMENT & MEDICINE ORDER MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Doctor Appointment & Medicine Order Management System is a digital platform designed to simplify healthcare services by allowing patients to easily book appointments with specialist doctors and order medicines online. Users can register, select doctors based on specialization and available timing, and receive confirmation of their booking. The system also enables patients to order prescribed medicines with transparent pricing, discounts, and home delivery options. An admin panel manages appointments, medicine orders, and user data efficiently. Overall, it enhances convenience, reduces waiting time, and provides a smooth and organized healthcare experience.
